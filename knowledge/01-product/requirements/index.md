# Product requirements

Requirements describe business intent, acceptance criteria, and rules. They may
come from Jira, Azure DevOps, documents, or other requirement sources.
They are the source of product meaning; implementation code is supporting
evidence and does not create business requirements automatically.

- [Login requirement](login.md) - A configured user can sign in.
- [File management requirement](files.md) - Authenticated users manage files according to role.
- [Authentication requirement](auth.md) - Users can authenticate and reach the protected workspace.
- [Folder management requirement](folders.md) - Authenticated users manage folders according to role.
- [Service health requirement](health.md) - The backend exposes a reachable health endpoint.
- [User preferences requirement](preferences.md) - Users manage supported appearance and profile preferences.
- [Role-based access requirement](rbac.md) - UI and API permissions remain aligned by role.
